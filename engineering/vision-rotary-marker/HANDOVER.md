# Vision — intermediate rotary marker, 2026-11-05

Branch `Patch_Feeding_Commissioning`. Operator confirmed a raw 0-degree sensor pulse near 90 in both directions, possible repeated intermediate pulses, and a stable 180 sensor exclusively at its end position. The actuator is pneumatic with two mechanical end positions. Operator accepted 1 s endpoint qualification for this commissioning arrangement. Preserve the assumption that normal intermediate pulses remain shorter than 1 s; a time-qualified magnetic signal is not encoder-based absolute position or proof against a mechanical jam.

## Implementation

Three source objects changed: `FB_IndexedPatchPressMachine`, `FB_VisionStation`, and `FB_VSCamera.RequestTrigger` (nine declaration/member sections). Main scan order, public machine interfaces, persistent DUTs, I/O links, drive parameters, HMI symbol names and TwinSAFE remain unchanged. Vision.Update intentionally gained raw rotary and capture-accepted inputs; its single caller was migrated. Existing unrelated Patch/other source changes were preserved.

Machine-owned TONs qualify physical end feedback for `tVisionRotaryConfirm := T#1S`. Qualification requires opposite raw sensor FALSE, safety/air and commissioned tooling, no reset and no opposite previous-scan final valve command. Unreferenced raw 0 alone cannot establish HOME: a command toward 0 must first establish its qualification, which can then remain valid while raw 0 remains active. This avoids accepting a long high before HOME has commanded the two-position actuator toward its end. Qualified feedback is shared by automatic positioning, HOME, Manual movement completion, Run Sequence permissives, tooling-home status and HMI position fields. The raw input records remain unchanged and available for diagnostics.

`FB_VisionStation` separately consumes both raw inputs. Raw 0 rising edges are sampled cyclically. Step 90 now requires qualified 0 before entering the flip. On step 100 entry, a valid 0 reference is retained; raw 0 must then be continuously FALSE for `tFlipDepartureFilter := T#20MS` before arming the intermediate marker. The first subsequent raw 0 rising edge, while raw 180 is FALSE, latches the marker. Additional pulses cannot clear/restart the timer or rearm the capture during that flip.

`fbVision.tTakingPictureWHileRotating` is now **T#100MS after the marker**, replacing the previous 300 ms elapsed-from-step-entry setting. The old variable name was retained for diagnostics. This is an initial commissioning value, not a guaranteed 115-degree exposure angle. Command output, PLC task/communication latency, camera latency and residual angular-speed variation still matter. The PLC task is 10 ms; verify actual pulse widths and low departure interval on the mechanism.

At delay expiry with program 1 selected, a request latch stays active through the camera handshake/result. A one-scan pulse would abort the existing camera FB handshake and was not introduced. Latches/timers clear on reset, successful new process start, exit from the flip, stop, disable or safety interruption. The return through the 90 marker does not arm a moving-picture capture because tracking is limited to outbound step 100.

The machine camera bridge gates an unacknowledged program-1 request when raw 180 is already active, preventing a queued late trigger. A capture acknowledged in that scan, or already acknowledged earlier, may finish its result/error recovery. `RequestTrigger` now accurately returns capture acceptance during accepted-error identification/recovery (state 35 or a captured-for-trigger result); trigger outputs and recovery actions are unchanged. This keeps 10130/40008 product-FAIL recovery working after physical 180 is reached.

If the marker/reference is missing, the camera program is wrong at the due instant, or physical 180 arrives before capture acceptance, the station produces a specific rotation-reference/capture-window fault (process fault detail 12), cancels the photo and does not substitute a late end-position image. This mechanical/synchronization failure is distinct from the existing recoverable accepted-image evaluation errors. Existing generic Vision fault event numbering is unchanged. Diagnostic detail is retained in `sDiagnosticInternal` / result detail.

Step 100 now requires qualified 180 and program-2 preparation/result conditions before advancing to the third inspection. The previously inactive physical target is active. Step 120/Home/Manual 0 movement keeps the valve requested through transient intermediate pulses, until final 0 has qualified for 1 s. Raw both-end sensor conflict supervision remains active rather than being hidden by qualification. Existing vertical/nest and opposing-output interlocks remain as inspected.

## Review and limits

This is the explicitly requested engineering change to endpoint timing and camera timing. It does not establish a safety-rated position measurement. With this two-magnet scheme, a stalled actuator that holds an ambiguous raw 0 for longer than the qualification time can still be misidentified; verify the operating envelope and mechanical behavior. The stable, exclusive 180 sensor supplies the unambiguous end reference the operator described. Do not treat timer stability as independent angular measurement.

Separately observed pre-existing issue: the machine's individual Manual rotation permissives have their nest-DOWN condition commented out. This increment did not reinstate or weaken that condition incidentally. Review its intended mechanical clearance separately before extending manual operating permissions.
